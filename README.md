# Student Grievance Redressal System

A web-based student grievance management system developed during my internship to help students register, track, and manage grievances through a structured workflow.

## ✨ Features

- 👤 Separate Student, Admin, Department, and Office modules
- 📝 Student registration and grievance submission
- 🗂️ Grievance categorization
- 🔄 Grievance assignment to the appropriate Department or Office
- 📊 Grievance status tracking
- 🔎 Students can view the status of submitted grievances
- 📈 Dashboard analytics for grievance management
- 🛠️ Department and Office users can update and resolve grievances

## 🔄 Grievance Flow

```text
Student
   ↓
Submit Grievance
   ↓
Admin
   ↓
Assign to Department / Office
   ↓
Department / Office
   ↓
Update / Resolve Grievance
   ↓
Student
   ↓
View Grievance Status

## 📊 Dashboard

The system provides grievance-related information such as:

- Total grievances
- Pending grievances
- In-progress grievances
- Resolved grievances

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Student registers and submits a grievance.
2. Admin reviews the grievance.
3. The grievance is routed to the appropriate Department or Office.
4. The assigned team updates the grievance status.
5. The student can track the progress and resolution.
